Sloosh V4, every piece on one page

Two layers: the Figma product, precise and untouched, and a hand-drawn ink layer on top. Every section runs in the same order: primitives, then tokens or components, then where they're used.

Base

The Figma product: nav, tiles, keycaps, composer, fields. v3 values, untouched. Precise. Never wobbles, never tilts, never handwritten.

Ink

A transparent drawing layer above the base. Arrives by being drawn, then coloured, then given a face. Leaves faster than it came.

Ink

The hand-drawn layer: eleven marks and one hand, drawn over the base, never part of it.

Primitives

Pen widths

Fine 1.2 · pen 1.6 · bold 2.1 · yellow 2.2. Screen pixels, never scaled.

Roughness

1.1 structure: skeletons, boxes. 0.4 faces: eyes and mouths stay cute. 1.5 fast marks: rings. Above 2 looks broken, below 0.6 looks like vector.

Usage

Ink lives in four places only. A mark that isn't doing one of these jobs gets cut.

The pointerNub, a doodled arrow with the logo's eyes. Points, asks, acts.
The flockSeven inked critters, each with one job: loaders, empty states, toasts, plan cards.
MomentsWaiting, arriving, succeeding, first run. Sketch skeletons, pencil progress, celebrations.
ReactionsBrackets on hover, a drawn ring on selection, drawn checks. Hover the tile.
Placement

Beside, around or below a target, never on top of it. Rings sit outside the box. One ink event per region. Everything un-draws at 40% of its draw time.

Registers

Making is Studio. Arriving, celebrating or explaining is Stage. Money is Billing.

RegisterWhereInk allowed
StudioComposer and feed, Spaces run form, Canvas, Assets, SettingsReactions and the pointer. One critter at most, only in a moment. None on Canvas.
StageExplore banners, Docs, onboarding, plan success, launchesEverything: ambient critters, handwritten asides, the ink logo.
BillingPricing, top-up, payment, invoices, gatesNo critters except the plan flock. The pointer may point at the best allowed option. Never a joke.
Do
  • Build the Figma screen first, then add ink in the four places
  • Draw a ring beside or around the target
  • Keep one lead critter per view
  • Write six words or fewer in the hand
  • Show every state in text or fill as well as in ink
  • Use brand-accent-text for yellow lines and yellow text
Don't
  • Restyle base components with wobbly borders or tilts
  • Dim the page or spotlight the target
  • Stack Sloo, Burb and Purl in one Studio screen
  • Put a paragraph, a price or an error in handwriting
  • Leave rings and trails on screen after their moment
  • Draw #FECC15 lines on white

Open questions

Need a decision before V4 is final. Most important first.

  1. Hugeicons settingsStroke weight and sizes still to add.
  2. Bowlby OneOne weight and reads heavy. Keep it, or pick a display face with weights?
  3. Gaps off the spacing scale144, 40 and 22–28 are in use but aren't spacing primitives.
  4. Radii without tokens28 for cards, 18 for the FAQ.
  5. Phone layers 3 and 4 match in darkBoth resolve to neutral-700. Pick a different step for one of them, or merge them.
  6. Older app vs DS4: buttonsRadius 8 vs 12, press scales to 0.97 vs drops by the lip, weight 500 vs 600, ghost hover fills vs colour only, disabled at 50% vs muted fill.
  7. Older app vs DS4: fields40 with a 1px edge on the page background vs 36 with a muted fill.
  8. Older app vs DS4: dialog, toast, composer, tab barDialog radius 20 vs 16. Toast on layer-3 vs card. Composer bordered at radius 20 vs flat at 16. Tab bar 61 vs 98.
  9. Older app vs DS4: generating and linksPulsing tile vs eyes and pencil progress. “View all” as a ghost button vs a text link. Focus as an outline vs the focus shadow.
  10. Which pricing rules go system-wide?Parked. The 40px first-section minimum, 12px caps and 144px around transitions stay pricing-only for now.
