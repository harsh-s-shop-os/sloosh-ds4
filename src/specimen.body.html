Sloosh V4, every piece on one page

V4 keeps the Figma product exactly as it is and draws a living layer of pencil on top. This page shows both layers with the real engine, so everything here moves the way it will in the product. Sources: the V4 zip (DESIGN.md, tokens, SlooshInk engine) and the v4 pricing prototype.

Base

The Figma product: nav, tiles, keycaps, composer, fields. v3 values, untouched. Precise. Never wobbles, never tilts, never handwritten.

Ink

A transparent drawing layer above the base. Arrives by being drawn, then coloured, then given a face. Leaves faster than it came.

Ink lives in four places only

If a mark isn't doing one of these four jobs, it gets cut.

The pointerNub, a doodled arrow with the logo's eyes. Points, asks, acts.
The flockSeven inked critters, each with one job: loaders, empty states, toasts, plan cards.
MomentsWaiting, arriving, succeeding, first run. Sketch skeletons, pencil progress, celebrations.
ReactionsBrackets on hover, a drawn ring on selection, a burst on make-actions, drawn checks. Hover the tile.

What building the pricing page decided

These rules came out of shipping the v4 pricing page. Where they disagree with older notes on this page, these win, and the sections below have been updated to match. Rules marked pricing only still need a call before they go system-wide.

One type scale

Eight sizes, plus 60 held in reserve. Nothing under 12px, no weight under 500, and 12px is always caps. Line heights are percentages, not pixels: 120% for titles, 140% for everything else. Every old size snapped to the nearest step; a tie went up (15 → 16, 18 → 20, 44 → 48). The pricing page went from 28 sizes to these.

Colour

Dark studio, one yellow, cream ink

Names and values are the Sloosh app's (packages/ui): primitives as HSL triples, the semantic set on top (background, card, muted, foreground, border…), the brand accent from its [data-brand="sloosh"] block and the layer ladder from the mobile prototype. Read live, so they flip with the theme switch. Swatches marked DS4 are not in the app yet: ink, stickers, data types, the glow and yellow-600.

Yellow budgetabout 2% of a Studio screen

A drawn ring counts as yellow. If a screen already shows a primary keycap and the credit chip, the ring goes on the keycap or nowhere.

Yellow strokes vs yellow fillscontrast

Fills use brand-accent (yellow-500 #FECC15) in both themes. Lines and yellow text use brand-accent-text: yellow-400 #FAC01A on dark, yellow-700 #AF8705 on light. The app is dark-only and keeps yellow-400; the light value is DS4's. Eyes never change colour.

Grounds and edges · from the prototype

Black, yellow, white, joined by sloosh edges

The prototype stacks whole sections on three grounds and joins them with a wavy edge instead of a straight cut. Each ground re-maps the tokens, so buttons and ink stay readable on it. These stay fixed in both themes.

Shipped rule: the first join (plan cards → "Which plan fits") is the model tape, a white strip tilted −2.2°, with no wave. Every other join into or out of yellow is a wavy edge (56px band, 28 on phones; top and bottom paths differ). Content keeps 144px clear of the tape or wave on both sides (96 on phones).

One size scale for every style: 24, 28, 32, 36, 40, 48 (data-size). Primary, secondary, outline, ghost, tinted and ink all take all six. Radius 12 at every size (at 24 that reads as a full pill). Labels: 24 is 12px caps; 28 to 36 are 14px; 40 and 48 are 16px; all semibold. The lip grows with height: 2px up to 36, 3px at 40, 4px at 48, and the press drops by the lip. The ink language

Eleven marks, one hand

Every hand-drawn thing in Sloosh is built from these. Lines wobble a little and never repeat exactly. Click a cell to draw it again.

Pen widths

Fine 1.2 · pen 1.6 · bold 2.1 · yellow 2.2. Screen pixels, never scaled.

Roughness

1.1 structure: skeletons, boxes. 0.4 faces: eyes and mouths stay cute. 1.5 fast marks: rings. Above 2 looks broken, below 0.6 looks like vector.

Placement

Beside, around or below a target, never on top of it. Rings sit outside the box. One ink event per region. Everything un-draws at 40% of its draw time.

Handwriting

The hand writes short

Caveat, written one letter at a time with a small random tilt: 38ms a letter for labels, 70ms for titles. Screen readers get the full text at once.

Icons

One icon library, no hand-drawn icons

The 28 hand-drawn doodles are retired (Oct 2026). Every icon now comes from the Hugeicons library already used in the product. The library link and the house settings (stroke weight, sizes) are still to be added here.

Where a doodle used to sit (banner megaphone, the "loved" heart on a tile, empty states, margin notes), use the matching Hugeicons icon, or nothing. Doodles are no longer drawn anywhere on this page.

The pointer

A second pointer that works next to you

It rests as two eyes in the top bar. When something matters it draws itself out, flies over, rings the target and writes a short tag. It never dims the page, never covers the target, never spends without a yes. Try the gestures.

Rules

How much ink, where

The test: if the screen is for making, it's Studio. If it's for arriving, celebrating or explaining, it's Stage. If money moves, it's Billing.

RegisterWhereInk allowed
StudioComposer and feed, Spaces run form, Canvas, Assets, SettingsReactions and the pointer. One critter at most, only in a moment. None on Canvas.
StageExplore banners, Docs, onboarding, plan success, launchesEverything: ambient critters, handwritten asides, the ink logo.
BillingPricing, top-up, payment, invoices, gatesNo critters except the plan flock. The pointer may point at the best allowed option. Never a joke.
Do
  • Build the Figma screen first, then add ink in the four places
  • Draw a ring beside or around the target
  • Keep one lead critter per view
  • Write six words or fewer in the hand
  • Show every state in text or fill as well as in ink
  • Use brand-accent-text for yellow lines and yellow text
Don't
  • Restyle base components with wobbly borders or tilts
  • Dim the page or spotlight the target
  • Stack Sloo, Burb and Purl in one Studio screen
  • Put a paragraph, a price or an error in handwriting
  • Leave rings and trails on screen after their moment
  • Draw #FECC15 lines on white

Open questions

Where the zip and the prototype disagree

These need a decision before V4 is final. Most important first.

  1. New: Hugeicons settingsThe library replaces the doodles; its link, stroke weight and sizes still need adding to this page.
  2. Bowlby One has no rules in DESIGN.mdStill open. The pricing page now uses it only on the type scale (48, 36, 28, 24) for titles, prices, plan names and big numbers. It has one weight and reads heavy; keep it, or pick a display face with weights.
  3. Prices: Bowlby or SF Pro?Decided on pricing: Bowlby One at 48 for prices, plan names and stats.
  4. Bowlby digits change widthStill open. Prices still shift sideways on the Monthly/Yearly toggle.
  5. The display size tokens carry heavy weightsSettled on pricing: Bowlby stays at 400 (its only weight); every other face has a 500 floor. The display tokens still say 700/800 and should be updated.
  6. The prototype's shapes sit outside the tokensStill open, narrowed: radius 28 (cards) and 18 (FAQ) shipped and need tokens. The 56px button was dropped for 48; the ink keycap shipped and needs a spec entry.
  7. The recommended plan has two treatmentsDecided: Pro is a solid yellow card with a black "Recommended" tag and a flat 4px yellow-700 band. No halo.
  8. Which pricing rules go system-wide?Parked for later. The 40px first-section minimum, 12px always in caps, and 144px around transitions stay pricing-only until then.
  9. Line heightsDecided: percentages, not pixels. 120% for titles, 140% for all other text, for now.
  10. Carousel bendDecided: stays at 7, on the pricing page only. Not part of the system.
